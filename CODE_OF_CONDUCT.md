# Rally Community Code of Conduct

Rally is built to help students. Everyone who participates in the project is expected to make the space welcoming, practical, and safe.

## Our standards

We welcome respectful feedback, good-faith questions, evidence-based corrections, and collaboration across different backgrounds, languages, and experience levels.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised language, threats, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission.

## Reporting

Stop engaging with harmful behaviour and report it privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that do not meet this code of conduct and may restrict participation when necessary.
